Colon Hydrotherapy

Understand the procedure before booking

Colon hydrotherapy uses water to flush the colon. Evidence does not establish general detox or wellness benefits, and the procedure carries risks. Ask your healthcare professional whether it is appropriate for you, especially if you have digestive symptoms or an existing health condition.
