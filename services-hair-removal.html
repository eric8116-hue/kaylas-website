Laser Hair Removal

Up to 95% permanent reduction

Laser hair removal uses a concentrated beam of light that is selectively absorbed by the pigment (melanin) in the hair follicle. That light energy converts to heat, which damages the follicle and inhibits future growth — targeting the dark pigment in the hair while leaving the surrounding skin unharmed.

Because hair grows in cycles, multiple treatments are required.

6–12 treatments typical All skin types No downtime

Additional treatments may be needed depending on ethnicity, the areas being treated, your hair type, hair growth pattern, and hormone imbalances.

The Detail

How laser hair removal actually works

  • Selective photothermolysisThe process behind laser hair removal, where the laser specifically targets melanin in the hair follicle.
  • Hair growth cycleNot all hair follicles are actively growing at the same time, so multiple treatments are needed to catch hair in every stage.
  • Melanin dependenceWorks best on dark hair with high melanin content, which makes it less effective on blonde or grey hair. That's where electrolysis comes in.
  • Treatment processThe device delivers pulses of light to the targeted area, causing a sensation often described as a warm pinprick.
  • Potential side effectsTemporary redness, swelling, and discomfort at the treatment site.

The Benefits

  • Reduced ingrown hairs — targets follicles directly, preventing hair curling back and growing inward
  • Improved skin texture — smoother, irritation-free skin, ideal for sensitive skin
  • Less painful — less discomforting than plucking hairs out by the roots
  • Longer lasting — permanently reduces growth, unlike shaving, waxing, or creams
  • Saves time and money — fewer razors, creams, and appointments over the years
  • Minimal side effects — most commonly redness at the follicle lasting 24–48 hours
  • No downtime — non-invasive, so you return to your day immediately
  • Helps other skin issues — can reduce inflamed bumps and itching

It also promotes smoother skin and prevents razor bumps, skin irritation, and reduces body odours.

Electrolysis

The only FDA-approved method for permanent removal of all unwanted hair

Where laser depends on pigment, electrolysis does not — which means it works on blonde, red, grey, and fine hair that a laser cannot see.

  1. A trained electrologist inserts a thin wire into the hair follicle.
  2. An electric current is sent down the wire to the hair root.
  3. The current destroys the follicle, preventing regrowth and causing existing hair to fall out.

Electrolysis can be uncomfortable and may require multiple sessions. The number needed depends on the person and the area. Clearing an area completely can take several months to a year, because there are three different hair growth cycles.
